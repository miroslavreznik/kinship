# Kinship Overclock credits

## Design and development

Game concept and direction: the Kinship solo project. Code, functional sound synthesis, temporary UI layout and rendering assistance: Codex. Engine: Godot 4.7.2, distributed under the MIT licence. Full engine and bundled dependency notices accompany the build in `GODOT-LICENSES.txt`; see also [Godot's licence and third party notices](https://godotengine.org/license/).

## Licensed external art and music

Luis Zuno / Ansimuz — Legacy Collection, GothicVania Town and Warped City environment and character art; ACTION PACK 1 `cyberpunk-street.ogg` music. Source: [Ansimuz](https://ansimuz.itch.io/).

Legacy Collection, GothicVania Town and Warped City art are supplied under their bundled CC0 licences. ACTION PACK 1 contains its bundled permissive licence. Copies accompany the build in `licenses/`. The exact selected source files are recorded in `asset-manifest.json` in the build and `game/assets/manifest.json` in the source project.

Characters: Terrible Knight, Bridge Heroine, Warped City player (future keeper). Environments: industrial parallax layers, sci-fi interior tileset, laboratory layers and laboratory support/tank props and the cyberpunk-corridor wall/vent sheet; GothicVania Town brick/wood tiles, windows, crates and barrels for the past workshop. Overclock and rejection effects use the Legacy Collection energy-shield and spark sheets. The family-memory photo reuses the licensed Bridge Heroine idle sprite inside a temporary frame drawn in code. The title landscape uses the original three-layer v2 forest from the supplied parallax forest pack. Room bays, mechanism shapes, ending panels, character lighting/palette treatments and sprite trails are rendered in code. No GothicVania village music by Pascal Belisle is used in this build.

## Original functional audio

Twelve functional effects — jump, dash, switch, blocked interaction, barrier break, relay, failure, warning, memory collection, captured pulse, mechanical work and completed work — were synthesized using deterministic DSP in `tools/make_sfx.py` and `tools/make_interaction_sfx.py`, with AI coding assistance. The supplied music is not AI-generated by this project.

## Temporary Lucky

Lucky is the MSI mascot. The user requested a temporary AI pixel-art sheet using the supplied reference; it was created with the built-in image-generation tool. The exact prompt is saved in `prototype-only/lucky-generation-prompt.txt`.

This sheet is a development draft, not eligible jam artwork. Final art must be independently human-created and the jam's restrictions require review before submission. MSI retains its rights in Lucky. This prototype is not an official or MSI-approved product. Official MSI reference images are not distributed in the build.

AI assistance covers code, tests, planning and story text, functional audio synthesis code, temporary UI and visual rendering treatments, and the separately identified Lucky draft.
